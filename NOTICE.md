# Acknowledgements

ChatVault was designed after studying **ChatGPT Bulk Exporter** by Vitalii Rizo:
https://github.com/vrizo/chatgpt-bulk-exporter (MIT license).

The original userscript demonstrated the approach to reading a ChatGPT conversation
list and exporting message data. ChatVault reimplements the core collector and ZIP
writer independently for a Manifest V3 extension, and adds scheduling and IndexedDB
snapshots. No files from the original repository are redistributed verbatim here.

ChatGPT is a trademark of OpenAI. ChatVault is independent, not affiliated with,
endorsed or sponsored by OpenAI.
